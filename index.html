About FleetHub

FleetHub is the local transport marketplace for Bulandshahr & Khurja district. One place to find every bus, tempo traveller and taxi operator near you — and call them directly.

For buyers: free, no app, no booking forms. See who's nearby, call or WhatsApp.

For operators: list your vehicles free from the Seller Login. Subscribers also get the AI Trip Cost Calculator to quote customers instantly.

Initial listings collected from public sources (Justdial, IndiaMART, operator websites). Operators can claim and manage their listing anytime.
